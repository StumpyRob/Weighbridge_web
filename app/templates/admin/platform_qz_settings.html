Scope{{ help(help_text.platform_qz_management_boundary, "platform-qz-management-boundary-help") }}

Platform admins manage QZ signing readiness once for the whole platform.

Tenant admins do not manage the certificate or private key. They only choose whether a destination prints directly to the workstation and which printer name to use.

Private key contents are never shown here. This page reports source, status, and diagnostics only.
